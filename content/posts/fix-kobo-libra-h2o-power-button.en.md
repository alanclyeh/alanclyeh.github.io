My Kobo Libra H2O sat unused for a while, so I assumed a dead battery. It charged and ran fine — but once it slept, nothing woke it. The power button did nothing, long press or short, and had no travel at all: no click, no give. Nobody online seemed to fix this, so I opened it up myself.

![Location of the power button on the back cover of the Kobo Libra H2O](/images/kobo/kobo_libra_h20_1.jpg)

## Removing the Back Cover

No screws — the cover is clip-on. Work a thin plastic shim around the edge and it pops loose. A guitar pick was exactly the right thickness: thin enough for the seam, soft enough not to scratch the bezel.

![Prying the clips loose from the edge of the back cover with a guitar pick](/images/kobo/kobo_libra_h20_2.jpg)

![Working along the side to continue releasing the back cover](/images/kobo/kobo_libra_h20_3.jpg)

## Finding the Culprit

The power button is a small module mounted inside the back cover. Whatever was in there had degraded — half-melted, soft and sticky. No wonder it couldn't trigger the switch on the board below.

![The power button module seated in position](/images/kobo/kobo_libra_h20_4.jpg)

## The Fix: Clean and Shim

Scrape out the perished rubber and the grime stuck to it.

![Close-up of the inside of the button](/images/kobo/kobo_libra_h20_5.jpg)

Inside the housing are two concentric rings. Cut a small round plastic shim to the height of the inner ring — enough for the button to press the switch below.

![The power button module cleaned and reseated](/images/kobo/kobo_libra_h20_6.jpg)

## Wrapping Up

Not a circuit failure — just a rubber dome that aged out and lost its spring. No shop would bother with it, and it's easy to do yourself. Writing it up in case it helps someone.
